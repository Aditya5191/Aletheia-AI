# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Data Scientists and ML Engineers auditing models, Product Managers exploring AI safety, and Compliance Officers checking for algorithmic bias and fairness.

## Product Purpose
To provide a platform for auditing AI models, testing them against various datasets, and generating compliance and fairness reports. It must bridge the gap between deep technical analysis and high-level compliance monitoring.

## Brand Personality
Accessible, Friendly, Simple.

## Anti-references
Overly dark, "hacker-style" neon UI that alienates non-technical users. The interface shouldn't feel like a terminal designed strictly for engineers.

## Design Principles
- **Clarity over Complexity:** Present deep technical data (like bias graphs and execution logs) in a way that is easy to digest for non-technical stakeholders.
- **Bridge the Gap:** Ensure the UX accommodates both deep-diving ML engineers and high-level Compliance Officers without alienating either.
- **Light & Legible:** Maintain an interface that feels open and approachable, avoiding aggressive neon or "cyberpunk" tropes that feel exclusionary.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
No strict WCAG requirements yet, but should follow standard best practices for contrast and readability.
